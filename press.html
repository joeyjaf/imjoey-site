Long bio (600 words)

Joey Fraser is the Head of Risk Operations at Fragile, a hardware-subscription platform powering recurring-revenue programs for some of the world's leading technology brands, and its consumer-facing sibling Whim.

At Fragile, Fraser oversees the entire risk and recovery function, leading strategy across underwriting, delinquency management, and loss mitigation. He joined as employee #5 in March 2023 and helped scale the company from $4,000 to over $2,000,000 in monthly recurring revenue, operating across merchant partnerships, customer success, risk, logistics, and underwriting as the team grew past 50 employees. He designed Fragile's underwriting framework from scratch — a system built on institutionally-defined risk vectors that was independently validated through data analysis and adopted company-wide as core risk infrastructure. Fraser also leads customer support and payment recovery for the Fragile and Whim portfolios, managing overdue subscriptions, payment commitments, return coordination, and escalation prevention. He has personally recovered more than $1.5 million in assets through direct risk operations, collaborating with legal counsel and a private investigator on high-stakes cases. He currently manages a direct team of eleven across risk, recovery, and operations and has hired more than twenty employees during his tenure.

Before Fragile, Fraser was Co-Owner and CFO of Project WiFi, an e-commerce management agency he helped scale from inception to more than 200 active client stores. Under his financial leadership, Project WiFi generated over $10 million in net profit across its portfolio and managed six-figure monthly receivables and expenditures. He oversaw operations for more than 100 domestic and international employees and sourced over $1 million in business from his personal network without paid acquisition. Earlier, Fraser worked as a credit scaling specialist in Los Angeles, helping more than twenty-eight clients secure over $1.1 million in business credit lines and facilitating 130-plus credit card approvals across 10-plus new business launches.

Fraser is also a real estate investor. He owns and operates 24 residential units in Waco, Texas, which he acquired and stabilized beginning in 2022.

Originally from Culver, Oregon, Fraser earned a Bachelor's degree in Business from Oregon State University in 2018, where he competed in Track and Field, served in Future Business Leaders of America, and participated in the OSU Ideation Station entrepreneurship program. He has also taught entrepreneurship at Falls City School.

Joey Fraser lives in San Francisco, California, and writes about risk operations and the subscription economy at imjoey.me. He can be reached at connect@imjoey.me.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Joey Fraser ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the personal SMS messaging service operated at the phone number +1 (806) 666-6490 (the "Service"). The Service is operated for personal, non-commercial use and is restricted to a small list of phone numbers authorized in advance by the operator.

1. Information We Collect

When you exchange messages with the Service, we receive and process:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The content of messages you send to the Service.
  • Message metadata provided by our telecommunications provider (timestamp, carrier, delivery status).

We do not request, collect, or store payment information, government identifiers, precise location, or other sensitive personal data through the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Verify that the sender is on the operator's pre-authorized allowlist.
  • Process the message content and generate a reply.
  • Send the reply back to the sender via SMS.
  • Maintain basic logs for security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

3. How Messages Are Processed

Message content is processed locally on the operator's hardware and may be passed to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. SMS transport is handled by Twilio, Inc., whose privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or message content with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information collected through the Service is shared only with the service providers strictly necessary to deliver and process the message (Twilio for SMS transport, Anthropic for message processing), and only as required by law.

5. Data Retention

Message logs are retained for a limited period for operational and security purposes and are deleted on a rolling basis. You may request deletion of your phone number and any associated logs by contacting us at the address below.

6. Security

The Service validates incoming requests cryptographically and restricts message processing to phone numbers on an allowlist. Reasonable technical and administrative measures are in place to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • You may stop receiving messages from the Service at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may request help by replying HELP.
  • You may request deletion of your information by emailing the address below.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
